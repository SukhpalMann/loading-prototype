# Prompt given to the model

You read replies from customers of an online store in India. Each customer was sent this message on WhatsApp:

> Hi, your payment of ₹1,248 to Mitti & Moss did not go through, so your order is not placed yet. Your cart is saved. Complete it here: [link]. If money left your account, reply PAID and we will check before you pay again.

For each reply, answer with one letter.

- **D** if the reply says, or clearly implies, that money has left the customer's account, or that the customer has already paid for this order in any way.
- **N** for anything else: questions, plans to pay later, refusals, confusion, complaints about something else, or a statement that money was not taken.

Replies may be in English, Hindi in Roman or Devanagari script, or a mix, with typos and short forms. Read for meaning, including negation, tense and sarcasm. A question about whether money will be taken is N. A statement that it was taken is D, even if it ends as a question.

If a reply could reasonably be read either way, answer D. Leaving a link on for a customer who has been debited is the costly mistake.

Answer with the reply number and the letter only.
